### 📌 Executive Overview

**QKD_Xplore Quantum Lab** is an advanced interactive web simulation engineered to break down the physical and procedural complexities of Quantum Key Distribution (QKD). Designed as an open-access experimental testbed alongside upcoming research literature, this platform bridges the gap between abstract quantum mechanics equations and physical optical bench setups. 

Through real-time 3D interactive graphics, dynamic polarization vector matching, configurable eavesdropper interception strategies, and automated Quantum Bit Error Rate (QBER) calculation arrays, QKD_Xplore transforms theoretical security theorems into verifiable, observable learning and research outcomes.
